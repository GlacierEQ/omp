- Router (AutoRouter, CoverageAwareRouter)
- Helix Orchestration (durable, persistent)
- Mission Batching
- Power Swarm (32 workers)
- MCP Integration (dynamic)

- cloud-deploy
- skills-extender
- key-manager
- persona-switcher
